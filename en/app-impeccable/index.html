What's inside

  • The price comes from your own numbers — your hourly rate, your minutes per bathroom, your minimum per visit — not an average.
  • You can quote homes, offices, Airbnbs, daycares or clinics, and it comes with three ready task lists: regular visit, deep clean and Airbnb turnover.
  • You quote, do the visit and take before-and-after photos with no signal, even in a basement.
  • You send the quote, the visit report with its photos or the invoice by WhatsApp or email.
  • Open it and start: no account, no password, and your clients and photos stay on your phone.

Who it's for

For anyone who cleans and runs their own business, alone or with help, as long as one person keeps the schedule. Impeccable does not share work between phones or take card payments; it does make the invoice.
