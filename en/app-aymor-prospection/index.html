Aymor Prospection

The enquiries that come in, sorted by themselves: who is worth the trip, who is waiting for an appointment, and what it is worth.

The app is in English, Spanish, French.

What's inside

  • A conversation page of your own, that you put on your site, your Instagram or your WhatsApp auto-reply — in English, French and Spanish, and it recognises which one they write to you in.
  • A score from 0 to 100 with its reasons written out in words, not a bare number. And it corrects itself with what the person actually bought from you.
  • A diary where you drag an appointment to move it, and which checks first whether it fits.
  • Quotes with the taxes of where your business is, not Quebec's for everybody.
  • The sheet for each job: what was done, by whom, what you charged, what you spent and what was left.

Who it's for

For service trades that take enquiries and lose time working out which ones are worth it. The questionnaire, the score, the appointments and the quotes are the house's own arithmetic: if the artificial intelligence falls over, the desk keeps asking its questions.
