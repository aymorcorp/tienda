What's inside

  • 409 recipes, the same ones in Spanish, French and English, 344 with a photograph; among them Mexican, Lebanese, French, Spanish, Greek, Portuguese and vegetarian cooking.
  • Type what you have at home and the app shows what you can cook right now and what is one or two ingredients short.
  • The shopping list subtracts what you already have in the pantry on its own, even when the measures do not match.
  • One button builds the week's menu: your favorites first and whatever uses up what you already have, starting with what is about to spoil.
  • No ads, no account, no password: your recipes stay on your phone.

Who it's for

For people who cook at home and want to plan the week. The app does not import recipes from web pages and has no timer, and your data does not move to another phone.
