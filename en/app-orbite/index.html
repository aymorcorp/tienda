What's inside

  • The “Today” screen shows only the people who are due for follow-up.
  • Each record carries a colored stage — to contact, contacted, following up, proposal sent, client — so you see at a glance who is where.
  • Orbite keeps four kinds of record: clients, employees or candidates, suppliers and network contacts.
  • A map shows who is nearby, and a button reads a record aloud so you can listen on your way to the meeting.
  • Open it and the list is there: no account, no password, and Orbite works without internet except for the map.

Who it's for

For people who work for themselves, sell a service or visit businesses and follow up. Not for teams: the data lives on one phone, and Orbite does not connect to your email or to Excel.
