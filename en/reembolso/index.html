Cancellation and refunds

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Cancelling

You can cancel at any time from your account in our store or in the Paddle portal: the link is in your receipt email. After you cancel you are not charged again and you keep access until the last day you paid for.

Refunds

You can ask for a refund within 14 days of your purchase. And if the law where you live gives you more, the law applies. Paddle, which processes the payment and is the merchant of record, applies its own refund policy (paddle.com/legal/refund-policy). To request a refund from Paddle, go to paddle.net (the link is in your receipt email).

If you bought in the App Store

If you bought on the App Store, Apple decides and processes the refund under its own rules.

A problem with the application

Write to us first at aymorcorp@gmail.com.

If you buy as a consumer

If you buy for personal use and live in Quebec, you also have the rights that Quebec's Consumer Protection Act gives for distance contracts, and the legal warranty. No clause of these terms takes them away.
