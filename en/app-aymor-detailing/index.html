Aymor Detailing

Quote, schedule, document with photos and collect on your detailing jobs, from your phone.

The app is in English, Spanish, French.

What's inside

  • The price comes from the vehicle size and the services you set in Settings, with your discounts, extras and taxes.
  • With your own link, your client picks the vehicle size and services, sees the price with taxes, sends up to four photos and asks for a day; it works once you upload your prices.
  • You make the invoice, the deposit, the balance and the receipt; your clients' money goes to your account, with no commission on your jobs.
  • Quoting, the schedule, job photos and payments work with no signal.
  • Aymor Detailing comes in Spanish, English, French, Portuguese and Italian, with each country's currency and taxes.

Who it's for

For auto detailers, solo or with a crew. The app does not run cards: you collect with your PayPal, your bank transfer or your payment link.
