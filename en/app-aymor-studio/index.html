What's inside

  • You put your link on Instagram or your site: whoever opens it talks to an artificial-intelligence assistant that asks their name, session type and date, and answers with your packages.
  • With your reception link turned on, it answers at night and on weekends, even with your phone off.
  • The quote carries your taxes and your policy; the deposit and the balance of each session are tracked separately, and invoices come out numbered.
  • You collect with your own PayPal, Stripe or bank transfer: your clients' money does not pass through us.
  • Studio starts with ten session types and their add-ons already set, with example prices you can all change.

Who it's for

For photographers and small studios. Studio is not a gallery and does not store your photos, the contract is not signed inside the app, and it sends no bulk email.
