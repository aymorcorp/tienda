Pricing

Last updated: 13 de octubre de 2026

Every product shows its price on its own page, with everything it includes. Here is what applies to all of them.

Which currency

All prices are in US dollars (USD). If your card is in another currency, your bank does the conversion.

Taxes

Any taxes that apply to your country or province are calculated and shown at checkout, before you confirm. The total you see is the total you are charged.

How often you are charged

Applications are subscriptions and renew by themselves, monthly or yearly depending on what you choose, until you cancel them. Guides and books are a single payment and do not renew. Each page says so.

We tell you before we renew

We tell you between 30 and 45 days before every yearly renewal, and between 7 and 30 days before any price change.

Cancelling

You cancel whenever you want, in one click, from “My subscription”. Without talking to anyone. After cancelling you keep access until the last day you paid for.

Refunds

You can ask for a refund within 14 days of your purchase. And if the law where you live gives you more, the law applies.
