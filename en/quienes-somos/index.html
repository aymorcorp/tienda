Who we are

A small house in Quebec making tools for people who work with their hands and their head.

We started because we saw the same thing again and again: good businesses, run by people who know their trade, fighting with software built for companies with a thousand employees. Expensive, in a language that is not theirs, with a hundred buttons of which they use three.

We do the opposite. Each application does one thing and does it completely. If you need to keep your crew's hours, that is what it does — it does not ask you to learn a payroll system.

And we work in the language you work in. Everything we make is in French, English and Spanish, and we are taking it to Portuguese and Italian. Not for decoration: because a business should not have to read in a foreign language to get paid for its work.

What you keep is yours. Almost everything we make lives on your own phone, not on a server of ours. We do not see your data, we do not sell it, and the day you leave you do not have to ask our permission to take it: you already have it.

We are few and we go slowly on purpose. We would rather release one application that works than three that almost do.

Aymor Applis, in Quebec, Canada.
