Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to install anything?

No. Quorum runs entirely in your browser — on a phone, tablet, or computer. You can save a shortcut to your home screen so it feels like an app, but there's nothing to download from a store.

Does Quorum work without internet?

You need internet to open the app the first time. After that, you can install it to your home screen and it keeps working offline — entering hours, calculating totals, everything except sending the report by email, which needs a connection.

Can I use Quorum on my phone and my computer at the same time?

Not yet — each device keeps its own data separately in this version. Multi-device sync is coming in a future version of Quorum.

What if I lose my phone or clear my browser?

Since your data lives only on that device, it's important to download a backup regularly (one tap, from the Settings screen) and keep it somewhere safe, like your own cloud drive.

Is location tracking mandatory?

No — it's completely optional and off by default. If you turn it on, it simply flags whether a clock-in happened near your business address. It never blocks anyone from clocking in.

Who is Quorum for?

Small and mid-sized businesses that manage a mix of staff, contract, and agency workers — and want a clean hours report without paying for a full payroll platform they don't need yet.
