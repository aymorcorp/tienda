Cancellation and refunds

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Cancelling

You can cancel at any time by writing to aymorcorp@gmail.com. After you cancel you are not charged again and you keep access until the last day you paid for.

Refunds

Amounts already charged are not refunded, unless the law where you live requires it. Paddle, which processes the payment and acts as the authorized reseller, applies its own refund policy (paddle.com/legal/refund-policy). To request a refund from Paddle, go to paddle.net (the link is in your receipt email).

If you bought in the App Store

If you bought on the App Store, Apple decides and processes the refund under its own rules.

A problem with the application

Write to us first at aymorcorp@gmail.com.

If you buy as a consumer

If you buy for personal use and live in Quebec, you also have the rights that Quebec's Consumer Protection Act gives for distance contracts, and the legal warranty. No clause of these terms takes them away.
